Retrieval-Augmented Generation: Methods and Open Problems

We organise recent retrieval augmented generation systems by retrieval stage, fusion strategy and generation recipe, and list the evaluation problems that remain open.

Architectures
Systems differ in whether retrieval happens once before generation, at every decoding step, or iteratively, with a critique model deciding when the evidence is sufficient. Hybrid retrieval that fuses a sparse BM25 ranking with a dense FAISS ranking is the most common default because it does not depend on the retriever being well trained.

Evaluation
Retrieval is measured with recall at k and mean reciprocal rank on a labelled set, while the final answer needs faithfulness and citation accuracy measured separately. A common failure is a correct retriever paired with a generator that ignores the evidence, which retrieval metrics cannot detect.
